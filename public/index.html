Our Direction

Vision

To build a sovereign Saudi platform that anchors advanced manufacturing and drives defense innovation.

Positioning the Kingdom as a Global Leader

We are committed to strengthening national capability, reducing external reliance, and positioning the Kingdom as a global leader in next-generation industrial and security solutions.

Our initiative aligns with Vision 2030 and supports the Kingdom's direction toward industrial localization, technological advancement, and economic diversification.
